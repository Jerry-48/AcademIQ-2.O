📖 AcademIQ User Manual

AcademIQ gives you notes, old papers, quizzes and a leaderboard in one place. Tap a heading to open it.

1. Create your account (first time only)
  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Create an account.
  2. Enter your 12-digit enrollment number (digits only, no spaces).
  3. Enter your full name and a password of at least 6 characters, then type the password again to confirm.
  4. Tap Register. You are signed in automatically.

Each enrollment number can be registered only once.

2. Sign in
  1. Enter your enrollment number and password, then tap Sign In.
  2. You stay signed in for up to 6 hours. After 30 minutes without activity you are signed out for safety (never in the middle of a quiz).
  3. Your account works on one device at a time. Signing in on a second device signs out the first.
3. Study notes
  1. Open Notes from the menu.
  2. Tap a semester chip (Sem 1, Sem 2, Sem 3 …) to see only that semester, then tap a subject chip. Use the search box to find a file and the sort menu to reorder.
  3. Tap View to read the PDF on the page, or Download to save it.
  4. Tap Add Note to share a PDF (max 10 MB) or a Google Drive link. An admin checks every submission before it appears, and you can send up to 5 per day.
  5. Need a new semester? In Add Note choose ➕ New folder… and start the name with the semester, for example Sem 4 - DBMS. After approval a Sem 4 chip appears for everyone.
4. Quizzes
  1. Open Quiz and pick a subject.
  2. Choose how many questions you want. Questions and answer options are shuffled every attempt.
  3. Tap one answer. It locks and shows whether it was right, then tap Next. You must answer each question before moving on.
  4. At the end you see your score and a question-by-question breakdown.

Only quizzes with 20 or more questions count towards the leaderboard. Shorter ones are practice.

5. Your Learning Report
  • After every quiz you get accuracy per topic, with a tag for each: Needs revision, Getting there or Strong.
  • A topic is a learning gap when you score below 50% on at least 2 of its questions.
  • For each gap, tap a recommended note to open it straight away, revise, then retake the quiz.
6. Leaderboard
  • Overall Top 10 adds up your best percentage in each subject.
  • Subject Top 10 ranks the best scores in one subject.
  • Participants shows how many students are using the quizzes. Your own row is marked "(you)".
  • New scores can take a few seconds to appear.
7. FAQ and troubleshooting
"Enrollment number must be exactly 12 digits"
Type only the 12 digits, with no spaces or letters.
"Incorrect password"
Check that Caps Lock is off and try again. If you have forgotten it, contact the admin (details below).
"This enrollment number is already registered"
Use Sign in instead. If you never registered it yourself, tell the admin.
"This enrollment number is not registered"
Tap Create an account first.
"Too many failed attempts"
Wait 10 minutes, then try again.
"Signed out on another device" or "session expired"
Just sign in again. This happens after 30 idle minutes, 6 hours, or a sign-in on another device.
"Unable to reach the server"
Check your internet connection and retry. If many students are online at once it can be slow, so wait a moment and try again.
A PDF does not show
Tap Open in a new tab under the viewer, or use Download.
Quizzes did not load
Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, tell the admin.
My score is not on the leaderboard
The quiz needs 20 or more questions, and the board can take a few seconds to update.
